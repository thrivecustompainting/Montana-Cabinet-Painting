The process

What your five days look like

Every kitchen is different, but most jobs follow this schedule.

  1. Day 1

    Protect & prep

    We mask counters, floors and walls, remove doors and drawer fronts, then clean and degrease every surface.

  2. Day 2

    Sand & repair

    We sand everything, fill dings and old hardware holes if you're changing hardware, and caulk the seams.

  3. Day 3

    Prime

    A bonding primer goes on the frames and on every door, so the finish grips and lasts.

  4. Day 4

    Finish coats

    We spray the finish coats for a smooth, brush-mark-free surface.

  5. Day 5

    Reinstall & walkthrough

    Doors, drawers and new hardware go back on, adjusted and aligned. We walk through it with you before we leave.

FAQ

Common questions

How long does a cabinet painting job take?

Most kitchens take about five days from prep to final walkthrough. Smaller jobs like a single vanity are faster. We'll give you a schedule with your quote.

Can I use my kitchen while you work?

Mostly, yes. Counters are covered during prep and spraying, but the sink, fridge and most appliances stay usable. We'll tell you which days are busiest.

Can you replace my knobs, pulls and hinges?

Yes. We install new knobs, pulls and hinges while the doors are off. If the new pulls don't match the old holes, we fill the old holes before painting.

What is nuDoor, and do you install it?

We're a certified nuDoor installer. If paint alone won't get you the look you want, we can go over nuDoor options for your cabinets during the estimate.

How durable is the finish?

We use cabinet-grade primer and finish coats made for high-touch surfaces, and we spray them for a hard, even film. Treat it gently for the first couple of weeks while it fully cures.

How do I get a quote?

Text us a few photos of your cabinets, wide shots plus a close-up of one door, and tell us your town. We'll reply with a ballpark and set up a free in-home estimate.
