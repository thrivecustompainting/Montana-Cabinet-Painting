# Montana Cabinet Painting: Brand Guide (v1.0)

Missoula, Montana · Cabinet painting · Hardware upgrades · New cabinet doors

The website uses the values below from `css/tokens.css`.

## 1. Brand story
Montana Cabinet Painting is a Missoula-based specialist that turns tired kitchen and bathroom cabinets into a smooth, durable, factory-quality finish without the cost or mess of a full remodel.

- **Promise:** A kitchen that looks new, done in about a week, at a fraction of the cost of replacing it.
- **Audience:** Missoula-area homeowners, landlords and realtors getting a home ready to sell.
- **Proof points:** ~5-day turnaround · sprayed finish · new knobs, pulls and hinges installed · new cabinet doors and drawer fronts · local and owner-operated.
- **Personality:** Craftsman, neighborly, straight-talking, careful with your home.

## 2. Voice and tone
Friendly, plain-spoken and confident. Use short sentences and real numbers. Name the rooms, towns and timelines people care about.

| Do say | Don't say |
|---|---|
| "Most kitchens are done in about five days." | "Revolutionary cabinet transformation solutions!" |
| "Text us a few photos and we'll send a quote." | "Best painters in the world, guaranteed!!!" |
| "We'll swap in new knobs, pulls and hinges while we're there." | "Fast" without a number |
| "Serving Missoula, Lolo, Frenchtown and the Bitterroot." | Jargon the homeowner has to look up |

**Taglines:** "Like-new cabinets in about five days." · "Paint, don't replace." · "Missoula's cabinet painting specialists."

## 3. Logo
The mark shows a Montana skyline above a single brass brush stroke. Pair it with the stacked wordmark: **MONTANA** (Inter SemiBold, wide letter-spacing) over **Cabinet Painting** (Fraunces SemiBold).

- Files: `images/logo-mark.svg` (forest), `images/logo-mark-reversed.svg` (for dark backgrounds), `favicon.svg`
- Clear space: half the mark's height on every side
- Minimum size: full lockup 1.25 in / 140 px wide; mark alone 0.375 in / 24 px
- Don't stretch it, recolor the brush stroke, add shadows, or place it on a busy photo without a solid panel behind it

This is a starter logo. If a designer makes a final one, keep these colors and fonts.

**Text contrast (WCAG):**
- Charcoal on Cream 12.4:1: any text
- Forest on Cream / Cream on Forest 10.4:1: any text
- Deep Brass on Cream 5.0:1: any text
- Charcoal on Sage 4.8:1: any text
- Charcoal on Brass 4.6:1: buttons, labels
- Brass on Forest 3.9:1: large text and decoration only
- Brass on Cream 2.7:1: decoration only, never text

## 6. UI elements
- **Contact actions, always in this order:** Text (primary, forest fill) → Call → Email (forest outline). Texting is the primary action because customers can send photos.
- **Buttons:** 4 px corners, Inter SemiBold, at least 44 px tall.
- **Badges:** brass outline pill with a brass icon, used for credentials and promises ("New doors & drawer fronts", "Done in ~5 days"). At most two per section.
- **Corners and lines:** small corners (4–6 px), like millwork. Use thin warm-gray rules (`#DDD3C1`) instead of heavy shadows.

## 7. Photography
- Before/after: shoot from the same spot, at the same height, with the same lens
- Natural daylight, lights on, no flash, phone held level
- Close-ups of the sprayed finish, door edges and new hardware
- Clear the counters; keep one or two styled items at most

## 8. Applications
- Business card 3.5 × 2 in: forest panel with the reversed mark, cream side with contact details
- Yard sign 24 × 18 in: forest background, "Cabinets painted here", brass phone-number bar
- Truck door: full-color lockup on light paint; reversed version on dark trucks
- Social post 1080 × 1080: before/after split with "Same cabinets. 5 days."
